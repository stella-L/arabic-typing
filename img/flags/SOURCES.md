# Flag image sources

Country flags in this folder are from `flag-icons`.

Source:
- https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons

License:
- MIT License

Used assets:
- `iq.svg`: Iraq flag, used for Baghdad and Iraq.
- `lb.svg`: Lebanon flag, used for Beirut.
- `kr.svg`: South Korea flag, used for Korea and "I am from Korea."
- `tn.svg`: Tunisia flag, used for Tunis.
- `sy.svg`: Syria flag, used for Damascus.
- `eg.svg`: Egypt flag, used for Cairo.

Note: country/city vocabulary should prefer flags when the target meaning is a country, capital, or major city tied clearly to one country.
